Customer Onboarding

How an onboarding moves through the bank

An asynchronous saga across bounded contexts: Outbox, Kafka and idempotent consumers.

  1. 1

    Application submitted

    Live

    The Customer Onboarding API commits the application and its OnboardingApplicationSubmitted event to the Outbox, in one transaction.

  2. 2

    Event published

    Live

    The Customer Outbox Publisher relays onboarding.application.submitted to Kafka, in order, exactly as committed.

  3. 3

    KYC case opened

    Live

    Customer KYC opens one case per application, scoped to the originating branch. Customer Onboarding moves the application to KYC in progress.

  4. 4

    Human KYC review

    Live

    A KYC officer of the branch verifies the identity and tax proofs. The first decision assigns the case to that officer.

  5. 5

    Outcome recorded

    Live

    KycCaseApproved / KycCaseRejected flows back; Customer Onboarding records KYC completed or rejected.

  6. 6

    Compliance and account opening

    Planned

    The next saga steps, with their own bounded contexts and human decision gates.
